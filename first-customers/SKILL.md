# First Customers

Help sell to the first 100 customers.

## Core Principle

**Skip the launch. Focus on selling.** "Viral success" is a myth. Every overnight success is built on months or years of hard work. Sell one by one, learn from each interaction, build momentum.

## The Concentric Circles of Sales

Sell outward from people who care most to those who care least:

### Circle 1: Friends and Family
- Pitch them as first customers, not investors
- They trust you most. If they won't buy, who will?
- Ask for honest feedback, not social media posts

### Circle 2: Your Community
- The community you've been contributing to
- Three steps:
  1. Make a list of everyone who has written/shared anything about a similar business
  2. Contact them all personally — walk through your product, do it hundreds of times
  3. Ask for candid feedback — not reviews, just honest input

### Circle 3: Strangers (Cold Outreach)
- Cold emails, calls, messages — this works
- Don't copy/paste. Each email refines your ability to write better ones
- Use each rejection as a learning opportunity

## Sales Reframing

- You're not convincing anyone — you're helping people
- You have a relationship with your community
- Turn every failed conversion into an insight
- Sales is education: customers get to know you, you learn what works

## Pricing (Charge Something!)

- Massive difference between free and $1 (zero price effect)
- **Cost-based**: Your costs + margin (20-50%)
- **Value-based**: What it's worth to the customer
- Start low, raise over time. Pricing is iterative.

## Key Metrics

- Manual sales = 99% of early growth. Word of mouth = 99% of later growth.
- Far fewer customers needed than you think. Slack's IPO: 575 customers = 40% of revenue.
- $10/month product → 200 customers → $2,000/month. One customer per business day = less than a year.
- **Product-market fit** = repeat customers who sign up and use on their own

## When to "Launch"

Don't launch until 100 paying customers. Then launch as a celebration of community support, not a customer acquisition strategy.

## Output

Help create:
1. 10 friends/family to pitch this week
2. 10 community members to reach out to
3. A cold outreach template (personalized, not copy-paste)
4. Initial pricing strategy
5. Weekly sales goal and tracking method
