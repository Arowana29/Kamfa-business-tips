# Marketing Plan

Build a marketing plan that starts with free, authentic content before spending money.

## Core Principle

**Marketing is sales at scale.** Don't confuse marketing with advertising. Marketing is about making fans, not headlines. Start by spending time, not money.

## Prerequisites

Before marketing, you should have:
- A community you belong to
- A product people are paying for
- ~100 customers (repeat customers = product-market fit)
- Experience selling one-on-one

## The Marketing Funnel

1. **Engage** — Encounter your content
2. **Follow** — Interesting enough to follow
3. **Research** — Check out your product/website
4. **Consider** — Evaluate pricing, features
5. **Buy** — Become a customer

You can't skip steps.

## Community vs. Audience

- **Community**: People who share interests with each other (you're a member too)
- **Audience**: Everyone you can reach when you have something to say
- Build audience to attract strangers → fans → customers

## Three Levels of Content

### Level 1: Educate
- Share what you've learned from building your business
- 100 customers = 100 things worth sharing
- Doesn't need to be polished — just consistent

### Level 2: Inspire
- Share your journey, struggles, successes
- Be vulnerable and authentic
- Document your progress, not just expertise

### Level 3: Entertain
- Hardest but most far-reaching
- Entertainment is king on every platform

## Social Media Guide

- Create two accounts: personal + business
- Be authentic. Focus on ideas, not self-promotion.
- Build in public. Share what you're working on.
- Pick one platform rather than juggling all.

## Email: Own Your Audience

- Social media = rented land (algorithms change, accounts shut down)
- Email = owned land (direct line, no algorithm)
- Start building an email list immediately
- Offer something valuable in exchange (guide, PDF, checklist)

## Spend Money Last

- Only spend on ads after organic traction and known customer profile
- Spend on customers (rewards, loyalty) before acquisition
- Don't pay more than you make per customer

## Output

Help create:
1. Primary content platform and posting schedule
2. 5 content ideas for each level (educate, inspire, entertain)
3. Email list strategy
4. "Build in public" plan
5. When (if ever) to consider paid advertising
