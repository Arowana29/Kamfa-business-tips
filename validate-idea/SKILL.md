# Validate Idea

Help validate a business idea before writing any code or spending money.

## Core Principle

**Validation happens through selling, not building.** Most founders spend months building something nobody wants. Validate by selling a manual version first.

## The Minimalist Validation Process

### Step 1: Define the Problem (not the solution)

- Who specifically has this problem? (Be precise — "freelance graphic designers who struggle with invoicing", not "businesses")
- How are they solving it today? (Current workaround = real competition)
- How painful is it? (Mild annoyance vs. hair-on-fire)
- Would they pay to make it go away?

### Step 2: Can You Solve It Manually First?

- Create a manual valuable process ("processizing")
- Do it yourself first. Write down every step.
- If you can solve it manually for a few people, you can eventually automate it.
- Example: Gumroad started as Sahil manually collecting PayPal info and paying creators one by one.

### Step 3: Will People Pay?

- Can you charge for this manual service right now?
- Have you talked to at least 10 potential customers?
- Have at least 3 said they'd pay (or actually paid)?
- What price point feels natural?

### Step 4: Four Questions Before Building

1. **Can I ship it in a weekend?** First iteration should be prototyped in 2-3 days.
2. **Is it making my customers' life better?** That's a minimum viable product.
3. **Is a customer willing to pay?** Profitable from day one.
4. **Can I get feedback quickly?** Faster feedback = faster iteration.

## Red Flags (Do Not Build)

- Nobody currently trying to solve this problem
- Can't name 10 specific people who have it
- Only validation is "my friends think it's cool"
- Need to educate people that they have this problem
- Building for a community you don't belong to

## Green Flags (Worth Pursuing)

- People already paying for inferior solutions
- You've manually solved this for a few people and they loved it
- Community actively complaining about this problem
- Can describe customer and pain point in one sentence
- Scratching your own itch

## Output

Clear verdict:
- **Validated**: Strong signals, proceed to MVP
- **Needs more validation**: Specific next steps to gather evidence
- **Pivot**: Idea needs fundamental changes — suggest directions
